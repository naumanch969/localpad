# Privacy Policy for LocalPad

Last Updated: March 26, 2026

## **1. Introduction**
LocalPad is a browser extension developed by naumanch969. This Privacy Policy describes how LocalPad handles your data. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## **2. What Data We Collect & Use**
LocalPad is built with a **"Privacy by Design"** philosophy. 
- **Personal Information**: LocalPad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email address, IP address, or location.
- **Project Data**: Any projects, notes, or port information you enter into the extension are stored **locally** on your device using the `chrome.storage.local` API. 
- **Browsing History**: LocalPad does not track your browsing history or monitor the websites you visit.

## **3. Data Transmission**
- **No External Servers**: LocalPad does not have a backend or any external server communication. 
- **100% Local**: All your project configurations remain exclusively on your local machine. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties.

## **4. Data Retention**
Your data belongs to you. You can remove all of your projects and notes at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing the extension's local storage.

## **5. Changes to This Policy**
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the "What's New" section of the Chrome Web Store entry.

## **6. Contact Us**
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub: [https://github.com/naumanch969/localpad](https://github.com/naumanch969/localpad)
